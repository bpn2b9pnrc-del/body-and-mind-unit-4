2️⃣ Vocabulary: Symptoms 🤒

🗣️ How do we talk about symptoms?

  • have got / has got + síntoma:
    I've got a headache.
    She has got a rash and a sore throat.
  • feel sick (sentirse mal):
    I can't eat. I feel sick.
  • hurts (duele) para una parte del cuerpo:
    My back hurts.
    Grandpa's knee hurts.
  • Con ache (dolor): stomachache · earache · toothache · headache
💡 Truco: a cough, a headache, a rash, a runny nose, a sore throat, a temperature ✅ pero earache, stomach ache, toothache ❌ sin "a".
3️⃣ Grammar 1: Object pronouns
📌 Regla 1: un object pronoun va después de un verbo (Look at him) o de una preposición (Talk to her).
📌 Regla 2: los object pronouns reemplazan a los sustantivos, así no repetimos el nombre. 🙂

Ejemplo: I like Anna. I talk to her.

4️⃣ Grammar 2 and 3: Can and Must

✅ Can for permission

📌 Usamos can para pedir y dar permiso.
👍 Afirmativo: We can go to the cinema.
👎 Negativo: They can't go to the party.
❓ Pregunta: Can she go to the concert? ➜ Respuesta corta: Yes, she can. / No, she can't.

🚦 Must / Mustn't

📌 must y mustn't van antes del infinitivo sin "to".
✅ Must = obligación (tienes que). ⛔ Mustn't = prohibición (no debes).
SubjectAffirmativeNegative
I / You / He / She / It / We / You / Theymust take the medicine.mustn't get out of bed.

✅ MUST = tienes que (obligación). Después de must va el verbo sin "to".

  • You must take the medicine.
  • You must stay in bed.

⛔ MUSTN'T = no debes (prohibición).

  • You mustn't get out of bed.
  • You mustn't talk to many people.
  • You mustn't eat any food today.
❌ No digas "must to take". ✔️ Di "must take".
